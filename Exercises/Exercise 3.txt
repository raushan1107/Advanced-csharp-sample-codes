Exercise: Multithreaded Stock Price Monitor
Topics: Multithreading, Tasks, Locks, Deadlock avoidance, Thread pools

Problem Statement:
Create a fake stock price monitor that updates and displays prices continuously.

Requirements:

A shared dictionary:
Dictionary<string, double> Prices
A background thread updates prices every 200ms.
A Task displays the prices every 1 second.
Protect access using lock mechanism.

Make sure it is deadlock proof:
Explain in comments why your lock ordering avoids deadlocks.

Expected Output:
TATA: 890.11   |   INFY: 1532.42   |   RELIANCE: 2550.00
